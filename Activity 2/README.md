This activity involved creating a brand identity for Riry Animations, including its logo, tagline, icons, color palette, typography, and business card.

I learned that personal branding is important because it shows who we are and what makes us different from others. I learned how colors, fonts, and logos work together to create a recognizable brand. My challenge was choosing design elements that matched the playful theme of Riry Animations. I solved this by using consistent colors, cute illustrations, and suitable typography. I can improve my branding skills by exploring more creative designs while maintaining consistency.
